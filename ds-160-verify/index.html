Add your supporting details card by card, upload the filled DS-160, and get a verified field report.

🔒 This page processes your most sensitive documents — passports and visas — and none of them leave your browser. Extraction, OCR and every cross-check run on this device. No file is uploaded, nothing is sent to any server, and nothing is saved after you close the tab.

Fill each card with what you have — upload a scan or paste the values. Then drop in the filled DS-160 Application printout from CEAC. VisaDash consolidates every entry, compares it to the form, and returns a verified report (matches + issues by severity).

What it checks. Names, passport number, dates of birth/expiry, home address & contact, prior visa foil, I-797 receipt/petitioner, I-20 SEVIS & arrival window, I-94 travel history, and internal DS-160 consistency. Findings are graded Blocker, Warning or Info — never “ready to submit”. Matching fields show as Verified.
How to get the cleanest results.
• DS-160: use the full Application printout from the CEAC review screen, not the one-page confirmation barcode page.
• Passport: a clear photo of the bio page, or paste the two MRZ lines from the bottom of the page.
• Born-digital PDFs compare instantly; scanned pages run through on-device OCR.
